Questions

Which states does it cover?

All of them: NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT and NT. Each has its own bond, notice and tribunal rules, and RentReady uses the right ones for your state.

Where are my photos stored?

On your own phone or computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you export a report and send it yourself.

Is this legal advice?

No. RentReady gives general information and templates based on official government guidance. For advice on your situation, contact a free tenant advice service in your state.

Can I rate my landlord?

Not in RentReady. For reviews of rentals and agencies, look at shitrentals.org.
