# Tenancy rules used by RentReady

Rules live in the `RULES` object in `index.html`. Each jurisdiction has a `checked` flag: `true` means its key rules were checked against an official page in November 2026; `false` means some rules come from general knowledge and the app shows a "rules still being checked" warning.

This is general information, not legal advice. Get every rule reviewed by a tenancy lawyer before public launch.

| State | Rent increase frequency | Notice | Challenge an increase | Bond held by | Bond disputes | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NSW | 12 months | 60 days | NCAT, within 30 days of notice | Fair Trading (Rental Bonds Online) | NCAT, 14 days after claim notice | Yes |
| VIC | 12 months | 60 days | CAV rent assessment, within 30 days | RTBA | RDRV, then VCAT; evidence 3 business days before claim | Yes |
| QLD | 12 months | 2 months | RTA dispute resolution or QCAT, within 30 days | RTA | Evidence within 14 days of claim; RTA dispute resolution, then QCAT | Yes |
| WA | 12 months | 60 days (not confirmed on source page) | Magistrates Court | Bonds Administration | Commissioner for Consumer Protection decides; appeal to Magistrates Court within 7 days | Mostly |
| SA | 12 months | 60 days | SACAT, within 90 days | CBS | SACAT | Yes (dispute deadline not confirmed) |
| TAS | Start/renewal of lease, or 12 months into a longer lease | 60 days | Residential Tenancy Commissioner | Rental Deposit Authority | Residential Tenancy Commissioner | No (bond process) |
| ACT | 12 months | 8 weeks | Above CPI + 10% needs tenant consent or ACAT order; review at least 2 weeks before start | Access Canberra | ACAT | Yes (bond process not confirmed) |
| NT | 6 months | 30 days | NTCAT | Landlord or agent | Form RT08, then NTCAT | No |
