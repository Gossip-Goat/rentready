# Tenancy rules used by RentReady

Rules live in the `RULES` object in `index.html`. Every jurisdiction's key rules were checked against official government pages in November 2026. The few details those pages don't state are listed under "Still to verify".

This is general information, not legal advice. Get every rule and template reviewed by a tenancy lawyer before public launch.

| State | Rent increase frequency | Notice | Challenge an increase | Bond held by | Bond disputes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NSW | 12 months | 60 days | NCAT, within 30 days of notice | Fair Trading (Rental Bonds Online) | NCAT, 14 days after claim notice |
| VIC | 12 months | 60 days | CAV rent assessment, within 30 days | RTBA | Evidence 3 business days before claim; RDRV, then VCAT |
| QLD | 12 months | 2 months | RTA dispute resolution or QCAT, within 30 days | RTA | Evidence within 14 days of claim; RTA dispute resolution, then QCAT |
| WA | 12 months (fixed term: only if the lease sets the amount or method) | 60 days, Form 10 | Magistrates Court | Bonds Administration | Commissioner for Consumer Protection decides; Magistrates Court appeal within 7 days |
| SA | 12 months | 60 days | SACAT, within 90 days | CBS (Residential Bonds Online) | Counter-offers with 10-day windows (up to 7), or escalate to SACAT; if tenant doesn't respond CBS decides |
| TAS | Start, renewal or extension of a lease, or 12 months into a longer lease | 60 days | Residential Tenancy Commissioner | Rental Deposit Authority (MyBond) | Dispute in MyBond; 10 working days for evidence; Commissioner decides; Magistrates Court appeal within 7 days |
| ACT | 12 months | 8 weeks | Above rental CPI + 10% needs tenant consent or ACAT order; review at least 2 weeks before start | ACT Rental Bonds | Tenant has 14 days to dispute the refund notice; disputes go to ACAT; undisputed part refunded |
| NT | 6 months, only if the agreement allows it | 30 days | NTCAT | Landlord or agent | Landlord must give notice within 7 business days with receipts and a statutory declaration (RT08), or return the full deposit; disputes to NTCAT |

## Still to verify

These are not stated on the official pages above:

- Deadlines to apply to the tribunal for a bond dispute in SA and the ACT, and to lodge a dispute in MyBond (TAS).
- Deadlines to challenge an excessive increase in WA, TAS and NT.
- Official condition report form names for QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT and NT (the app uses descriptive names).
